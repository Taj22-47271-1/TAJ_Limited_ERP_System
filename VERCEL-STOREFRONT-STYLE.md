# Customer Storefront Style Update

The customer-facing experience was redesigned into a minimalist commerce layout inspired by the visual language of demo.vercel.store while preserving TAJ Limited branding and all existing Laravel business logic.

Updated:
- Customer navigation/header with global product search and compact cart/account controls.
- Customer home with a large featured-product mosaic and product discovery grid.
- Shop grid with large product imagery, understated borders, product metadata, live stock and add-to-cart actions.
- Product detail page with large sticky product media and a simplified buy panel.
- Cart and checkout pages with minimal panels and black pill actions.
- Customer footer and account/order pages re-skinned for the same visual system.

No database migration is required for this style update.
